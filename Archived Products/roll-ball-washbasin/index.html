Roll Ball

sculptural washbasin

Roll Ball achieves what the sphere alone cannot — it stops. A spherical form resolved into a washbasin by a single, considered cut: the horizontal plane that creates the rim, the interior that follows the outer curve inward.

The Form

The Sphere,
Made Still

The sphere is in perpetual motion — it has no base, no top, no front. Roll Ball makes the sphere an object by giving it a place to rest: a flat base machined to provide stability, and a circular rim cut from the upper hemisphere to create the basin. The form is as simple as this. The execution is not.

Curvature & Craft

Curved Stone,
Perfect Radius

Machining a perfect hemisphere from a block of marble requires exceptional precision. The radius must be maintained without interruption from base to rim. Any deviation becomes visible in the finished object — the eye reads spherical geometry with unforgiving accuracy. Roll Ball's perfection is earned in the atelier.

A flat base area is machined into the lower hemisphere, providing a stable resting surface. The basin can also be specified with a custom cradle in matching or complementary stone for additional security and aesthetic completeness.

Standard outer diameter is approximately 380–420mm. Custom diameters — larger for dramatic scale, smaller for compact applications — are available on commission.

The interior follows the outer curvature for the majority of its depth, becoming slightly flatter at the base to accommodate the drain fitting without compromising stability. The visual reading from above remains fully circular.

Roll Ball is a countertop vessel basin by design. Its spherical form is intended to be read in full above the surface on which it sits. A custom plinth or cradle for freestanding positioning is available on commission.
