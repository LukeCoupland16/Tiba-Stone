Lavanche

marble coffee table

Lavanche is stone as arrested force. Its generous plane carries the full weight of the material — broad, unhurried, present. A piece that does not attempt to disappear into a room, but instead gives the room its centre of gravity.

Geological Character

Mass
in Motion

The Lavanche is a study in arrested force. Its proportions recall the geological processes that form marble itself — immense pressure accumulated over millennia, recorded in every vein that crosses the surface. The stone is chosen for the directional charge of its patterning: lines that move across the slab with a momentum entirely their own.

Design Intent

Weight
as Virtue

Where other designs minimise the presence of stone, Lavanche celebrates it. The depth of the top is substantial by intent — a proportional decision that gives the piece a gravitational authority in any room. It does not ask to be noticed. It simply is.

Yes. The standard Lavanche is 120 × 70 × 38 cm, but we regularly produce pieces to client-specified dimensions. The generous proportions of the Lavanche mean that scaling up or down is often a straightforward conversation. Share your requirements in your inquiry and our design team will advise on structural implications and slab availability for the stone selected.

The Lavanche is offered in polished and honed finishes. Polished surfaces accentuate the movement of the veining and are particularly striking in Calacatta Viola and Noir Aziza. Honed finishes give the stone a more tactile, matte character that suits Fendy Grey and Verde Patricia especially well. We can arrange sample dispatch to assist your selection.

For everyday care, wipe with a damp cloth and a pH-neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic substances such as wine, citrus, or vinegar. Given the generous surface area of the Lavanche, we particularly recommend sealing the stone upon delivery and annually thereafter with a quality impregnating sealer. Full care instructions are included with every piece.
