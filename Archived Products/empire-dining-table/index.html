Empire is available in configurations that seat six to fourteen comfortably. Standard lengths are designed around the typical 60cm per person allocation. For unusually large rooms or formal dining spaces, we have produced Empire tables at exceptional scale — the slab selection and logistics for these are handled with particular care, and we discuss them individually with each client.

In most residential construction, existing floor structure is adequate for a stone dining table. We recommend a structural survey for very large tables or for installations on upper floors of older buildings. Our team conducts a site assessment prior to delivery and will advise if any preparation is required. We coordinate with architects and structural engineers as needed.

Empire is delivered by specialist stone logistics teams. The table is typically transported and assembled in situ — base first, then top — to avoid issues with doorways and circulation. Access routes are assessed in advance. White-glove installation is included, and our team oversees the process through to final levelling and sign-off.

Yes. The standard Empire base is a solid stone trestle — an austere, powerful form that suits the scale of the table. For clients who need to maximise seating at the ends, or who prefer a more architectural base, we offer a solid slab base option and a metal trestle in brushed brass or powder-coated steel. These are discussed during the design consultation.
