Sfera

velvet bench on onyx spheres

Sfera brings together the softness of hand-upholstered velvet and the weight of hand-turned onyx spheres. The oval silhouette floats above four stone feet — a conversation between the yielding and the permanent.

Request a Quote
The Dialogue

Soft Above,
Stone Below

Sfera lives at the intersection of two opposing forces. The generous oval cushion — dense foam wrapped in sumptuous velvet — sits atop four hand-turned onyx spheres, each one unique in its veining. The contrast is the design.

Geometry & Gravity

Four Points
of Contact

Each onyx sphere is turned from a single block, then polished to reveal the stone's interior life. Set beneath the cushion at precise intervals, the four feet create a visual tension — weight held aloft by roundness, the heavy made to appear light.

Stone & Surface

The onyx spheres are where material selection makes its mark. Warm-toned stones — Rosso Levanto, Calacatta Viola — complement the emerald velvet with Mediterranean warmth. Darker choices like Noir Aziza create a more dramatic, contemporary contrast.
