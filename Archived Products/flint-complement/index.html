Flint

bespoke marble accent

Flint is named for the oldest worked stone — the one that first held an edge. Here, that ancient hardness is refined into an object that occupies a surface with absolute authority.

Request a Quote
Ancient in Memory

Ancient in Memory,
Precise in Making

Flint draws its character from the stone's geological weight — its density, its resistance to light, its refusal to be decorative. The object is made with precision tools, but it retains something older and more considered.

Darkness & Design

Darkness as a
Design Choice

Dark marble is not a neutral choice. Flint in Noir Aziza or Emperador occupies the room differently from a pale stone — it absorbs light rather than reflecting it, anchoring a surface rather than adorning it. This is the intention.

Flint is a surface object — most naturally a paperweight, a small anchor for a desk or reading table. Its density makes it satisfying to handle and its geometry makes it worth looking at. It has no other intended function.

Dark marbles show dust and watermarks more visibly than pale stones. Wipe with a dry or lightly damp microfibre cloth. Avoid acidic contact and direct sunlight, which can gradually bleach dark stones. We apply a penetrating sealer before dispatch.

Yes. Flint can be produced in custom proportions to suit a specific surface or use. Inquire with your requirements and we will confirm feasibility and lead time.

No two pieces of stone are identical. Swatches indicate the character of the stone — its colour, tone, and veining density — but the final piece will be singular. This is the nature and the value of natural marble.
