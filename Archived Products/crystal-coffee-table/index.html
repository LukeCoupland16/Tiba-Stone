Crystal

coffee table

Light finds its way through stone. The Crystal table is carved from a single slab of Calacatta Oro — its warm gold veining pooling like morning sun on still water. A surface that changes with every hour of the day.

Every Crystal begins as a monolithic slab — chosen by hand at the quarry for the particular depth and warmth of its veining. The tabletop and the plinth are cut from the same stone, ensuring the veining flows as one continuous composition from surface to floor.

The Geometry

The table's geometry is deliberately simple — allowing the stone to remain the subject. A slim polished slab hovers above a quietly sculpted plinth, its chamfered edges catching and bending light throughout the day. Stillness made material.

Stone & Finish

Calacatta Oro

The Crystal is offered exclusively in Calacatta Oro — chosen for its warm ivory ground and the sweeping amber veins that catch and hold the light. Each slab is hand-selected at the quarry to ensure the veining composition enhances the table's geometry. No two pieces are alike.

Available in polished or honed finish. The chamfered edge is always polished regardless of surface choice, creating a refined counterpoint between the two treatments. Custom sizing is available on request.

Yes. The Crystal is made entirely to order and can be produced in any dimensions you require. Round, square, and oval formats are available alongside the standard rectangular form. Custom heights are also possible to suit specific seating configurations.

A polished surface is mirror-like — it intensifies colour and veining, and reflects light dramatically. Honed is matte and satin-smooth, softer in character and more forgiving in everyday use. The Crystal's chamfered edges are always polished regardless of surface choice, which creates a refined counterpoint.

Each slab is chosen in person at our quarry partners in Carrara and Afyon. We look for a specific quality of veining movement — not simply dramatic patterning, but a particular flow and luminosity in the stone. For the Crystal, we share the shortlisted slabs with clients so the final choice is made together.

Standard pieces are produced in 12–16 weeks from order confirmation. Custom dimensions may require additional lead time depending on slab availability at the quarry. We will advise precise timelines at the inquiry stage.

Yes. Every Crystal is delivered and placed by our specialist handling team. Given the weight and fragility of natural stone, we do not courier our pieces — delivery is always supervised by trained installers. We operate worldwide through certified specialist logistics partners.
