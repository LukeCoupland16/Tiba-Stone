Gioco

velvet bench on onyx spheres

Gioco takes the playful tension between velvet and stone and gives it a more architectural frame. A rectangular emerald cushion rests on four hand-turned onyx spheres — the clean geometry of the seat playing against the perfect roundness beneath it.

Request a Quote
The Game

Straight Lines,
Round Feet

Gioco — Italian for game, for play — celebrates the visual tension between opposites. The rectangular cushion is deliberately geometric: crisp edges, taut corners, a ruled silhouette. Beneath it, four onyx spheres refuse all straight lines. The result is a bench that feels like a riddle, a piece that rewards the second glance.

Colour & Character

Emerald Depth,
Stone Permanence

The emerald green velvet carries the richness of old-world library interiors and the freshness of a forest canopy. Against the warm mineral tones of onyx, it creates a palette that is simultaneously classic and unexpected — a colour story that anchors any room it enters.
