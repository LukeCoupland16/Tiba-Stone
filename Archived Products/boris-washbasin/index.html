Boris

sculptural washbasin

Boris is built from considered angles — each edge a decision, each plane a resolution. The result is a washbasin of architectural authority that holds its position in any space without apology.

Boris begins where most washbasins end — at the corners. Each angle is cut with intention, each edge a conversation between the interior basin and the exterior mass. The result reads as sculpture first, washbasin second.

Boris's geometry is not merely aesthetic — it reflects the logic of the stone itself. Marble cleaves along natural lines; Boris works with those lines, making the cutting process legible in the finished object. Function and form arrive at the same answer.

Stone & Surface

Boris rewards high-contrast stone. The angular geometry creates shadow lines that shift through the day; stones with strong directional veining — Rosso Levanto, Noir Aziza — dramatise these transitions beautifully. More restrained stones deliver a quieter, more refined reading.
