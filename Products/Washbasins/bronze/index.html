Bronze

countertop washbasin

Bronze does not contain bronze — it evokes it. The warm, burnished quality of the chosen stone, the low profile, the carefully considered proportions: together they achieve the weight and warmth of something precious cast rather than carved.

The Form

Warm Mass,
Quiet Presence

Bronze is a washbasin that settles into its space. Its proportions are deliberately horizontal — a broad, low vessel that grounds itself against the vertical plane of the wall behind it. The warmth comes from the stone; the authority comes from the form.

Stone & Light

The Quality
Of Warm Stone

Certain marbles carry a warmth that no other material can replicate — an internal luminosity that changes with the light throughout the day. Bronze is designed to reveal this quality in full: its broad, open interior maximises the stone's surface, letting the material perform.
