Punta High

pedestal washbasin

Punta High rises to a point — not literally, but in intention. Every geometric decision moves upward, resolving in a basin that sits at the apex of a considered angular composition. Stone and geometry in complete agreement.

The Form

Angular Rise,
Precise Resolution

Punta High draws from the geometry of the pointed arch — the meeting of two forces at a single resolved line. The tall column gathers toward the basin with controlled angularity, each plane decided and deliberate. Nothing bends; everything arrives.

Geometry & Stone

The Point
Of Precision

The angular transitions of Punta High place particular demands on the stone cutter. Each facet is machined to tolerances that make the geometry legible at every scale — from the full-height silhouette to the edge detail at the rim. Precision is the material here as much as marble is.

Stone & Surface

Punta High's faceted geometry creates shadow plays that shift dramatically with light conditions. Darker stones — Beige Travertine, Silver Travertine — make these transitions most legible. Rosso Levanto creates a more warm, architectural reading. The choice of stone determines whether Punta High reads as cool or warm, dramatic or restrained.
