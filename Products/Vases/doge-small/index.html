The Vessel

A Volume
in Stone

The Doge Small is lathe-turned from a single block, its exterior polished to a full mirror, its interior left honed. The geometry is resolved — no ornament, no seam. It is the kind of object that improves with proximity: the closer you look, the more considered it becomes.

Scale & Intention

Presence
Without Scale

Small stone objects are not a lesser category. They are stone distilled — the weight and character of a material compressed into something you can hold. The Doge Small rewards placement on surfaces where scale is noticed: a desk, a shelf, beside a lamp.

Stone & Surface
