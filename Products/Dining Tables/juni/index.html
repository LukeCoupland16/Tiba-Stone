Juni is made to order and can be configured to suit almost any space. Standard dimensions are available as a reference; most clients order custom. Inquire with your room dimensions and seating requirement.

The standard base is integrated marble — carved as a continuation of the table's form. A metal base option is available for certain configurations. Both are available on inquiry.

All Tiba Stone surfaces are sealed and finished for daily use. Regular maintenance requires only a damp cloth. Avoid acidic substances. We recommend an annual re-seal for tables in active use.

Yes. We provide full stone samples for all dining table commissions before any work begins. The selection visit is part of our standard process.
