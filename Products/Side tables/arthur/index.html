marble side table

Some pieces are furniture. Arthur is a reference point — the still object in a room that everything else orients around. The Monolithic Form

Cut from One Stone

Arthur is carved from a single block of marble — top, pedestal, and base in one continuous form. No joints, no seams, no interruption. The veining runs from the polished surface straight down to the floor without deviation, mapping the same slab across the entire height of the piece. This is what monolithic means: an object that is also a record.

Proportion as Philosophy

Proportion as
Philosophy

A side table is the most considered object in a room. It exists at arm's reach, at eye level when seated — it is read at close range, every day. Arthur's proportions were arrived at slowly: the top generous enough to be useful, the pedestal narrow enough not to intrude, the base stable without appearing heavy. Nothing is ornament.

Polished marble has a high-gloss, mirror-like surface that amplifies the depth of veining and reflects light dramatically. Honed marble is matte — softer in appearance, more tactile, and less susceptible to showing scratches. Both are available for Arthur. Most residential applications choose honed; polished is common in more formal or dramatic interior settings.
