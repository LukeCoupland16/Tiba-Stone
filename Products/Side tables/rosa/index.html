Rosa

onyx pedestal side table

A single cylinder rises to meet a generous round top — pink onyx at its most elemental. Rosa distils the side table to its purest geometry: circle upon circle, warm stone catching the light from every angle.

Elemental Form

The Circle,
Twice Told

Rosa is the circle repeated — a cylindrical pedestal rising to a perfectly round top. The form is ancient, almost primal, yet in pink onyx it becomes something altogether contemporary. The translucency of the stone means Rosa shifts throughout the day, absorbing and releasing light like a living thing.
