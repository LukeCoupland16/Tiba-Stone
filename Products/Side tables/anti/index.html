Anti

marble side table

A slab of stone that appears to hover. The Anti contradicts everything marble is expected to be — imposing reduced to elemental, mass dissolved into line. One monolithic top, one precise iron pin. Nothing more is needed.

Request a Quote
The Concept

Stone Against Itself

Marble carries centuries of expectation: heavy pedestals, ornate columns, the weight of antiquity. The Anti refuses all of it. The top is a single unbroken slab, its edges honed to a knife-thin profile that makes the stone appear to float. The stem disappears beneath it — present only as a shadow on the floor.

The Craft

Iron as
Argument

The hand-forged iron stem is not a support — it is a statement. Dark, slender, almost aggressive in its simplicity, it holds the stone in quiet opposition. Two materials that should not agree — and yet they do, precisely because neither makes any concession to the other.

The top is a single unbroken marble slab with edges honed to a knife-thin profile — at 3 cm thick, the underside is chamfered so the stone appears to have no mass at its perimeter. The hand-forged iron pin sits centrally beneath, its dark slender form visually receding so the stone appears unsupported from most angles.

The standard finish is blackened iron — a raw, oxidised surface that ages gracefully. We also offer brushed steel and oil-rubbed bronze on request. The intent is always for the stem to recede rather than compete, so we advise darker finishes across all stone selections.

Yes. All Anti tables ship pre-sealed with a premium penetrating stone sealer. We recommend reapplication annually in domestic settings or bi-annually in commercial environments. A care and maintenance guide is included with every piece.

Every Anti is delivered by our specialist handling team in a custom-made crate. Delivery includes white-glove placement and final inspection at your location. Given the precision required for correct installation, we do not ship via standard courier — all logistics are managed directly by Tiba Stone.
