Onda traces the profile of a breaking wave — the moment before the foam, the curve at its most taut. A console shaped by a force that never holds still, held permanently in a material that never moves.

Water shapes stone over centuries. Onda reverses the process — stone shaped to hold the character of water at its most dynamic. The wave caught at its peak, the curl arrested before it crashes. An impossible moment made permanent.

The energy of a breaking wave is enormous — pure kinetic force at its moment of resolution. Onda asks marble to hold that energy without releasing it. The result is a console of considerable presence, containing a force it can never express.
