Curves

an organic marble console

Curves finds the logic of water in stone — the same forces that shape river stones and coastal cliffs expressed as a console for considered interiors. Where most furniture imposes geometry, Curves discovers it.

Request a Quote
Natural Intelligence

The Logic of the
Unhurried Line

A river does not choose its path. It finds the line of least resistance, and in doing so, arrives at beauty without intention. Curves applies this logic to marble — form arrived at through the material's own tendencies, not imposed upon it.

Form That

In marble, the organic line is doubly paradoxical — the hardest of materials holding the character of the softest forces. Curves makes this contradiction visible, and in doing so, shows something true about stone.
