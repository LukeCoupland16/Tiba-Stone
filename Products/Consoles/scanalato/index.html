Scanalato

fluted pedestal console

A single fluted column rises to support a generous surface — classical channeling meets modern restraint. The vertical grooves catch raking light and release it in soft gradations, giving the pedestal a rhythm that shifts throughout the day.

Request a Quote
The Column

Carved from
a Single Block

The fluted pedestal is milled from one continuous piece of blush-toned marble. Each channel is cut to identical depth and spacing, creating a rhythm of shadow and highlight that transforms with the angle of light. The result is a column that feels both ancient and unmistakably contemporary.

Surface & Structure

One Pedestal,
Infinite Light

Where the channeled base meets the smooth tabletop, two geometries converge without apology. The top extends generously beyond the column, creating a cantilever that defies the mass below. Pink marble warms every room it enters — Scanalato does so with architectural conviction.

Yes. The standard Scanalato features evenly spaced vertical channels, but the flute count and depth can be adjusted during consultation. Wider channels create bolder shadows; narrower ones produce a finer, more textile-like surface.

The pedestal is engineered with a substantial footprint and low centre of gravity. The mass of the marble itself — over 100 kg — provides inherent stability. A concealed steel plate within the base adds structural reinforcement for daily use.

All Tiba Stone pieces are sealed with a professional-grade penetrating sealant before delivery. For console use — decorative rather than wet-area — the surface is highly resistant to everyday contact. We recommend resealing every two to three years.
