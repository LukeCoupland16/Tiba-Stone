T-02

a precision marble console

T-02 is named as a prototype is named — its identity is its specification. An exercise in precision: tolerances measured in tenths, surfaces finished to a single plane, form dictated entirely by structural honesty.

Technical Precision

Form Dictated
by Function

T-02 takes its name from the engineering convention — a designation before a name, a specification before a story. The console is an exercise in structural honesty: no element that is not required, no surface that does not serve. The result is an object of considerable rigour.

Specification

In engineering, the prototype carries a particular beauty — the absence of styling decisions, the purity of function made form. T-02 asks whether that quality can be a design choice, whether the rigour of a specification can itself be an aesthetic.

T-02 is a prototype designation — the second in a series of technical studies. The name is retained because it accurately describes the piece: a form arrived at through specification rather than styling.

T-02 is at home in technically rigorous environments — architect studios, design offices, minimalist residences. Its character comes from precision, and it is most legible in spaces that value precision.

Surface finish and edge treatment can be specified. Enquire for a detailed technical consultation.
