Shan

a sculptural marble console

Shan is the Chinese character for mountain — upright, settled, enduring. A console that rises from the floor with the unhurried permanence of formations that have stood for millennia and will stand for millennia more.

Request a Quote
Mountain Logic

The Console as
Landform

In classical Chinese painting, mountains are not backdrops but presences — protagonists of the composition. Shan applies this understanding to furniture: a console that dominates its space not through scale but through absolute stillness.

Permanence & Proportion

Settled,
Unmoveable

The mountain does not assert itself. Its authority comes from permanence — the certainty that it was here before you arrived and will remain after you leave. Shan carries this quality into the interior, offering a different quality of time.
