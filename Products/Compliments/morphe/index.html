Morphe

composed marble object

From the Greek for form — the thing before it has a name. Morphe is a composed marble object: two pieces of stone, met and fixed in permanent relationship.

Request a Quote
The Joint

Where Stone
Meets Stone

The joint between the two pieces of Morphe is its defining moment. Two precision-cut faces are brought together dry — no adhesive, no filler — and fixed in permanent contact. The line between them becomes the object's most considered detail.

Form & Intention

Form Before
Function

Morphe makes no pretence of utility. It exists to be looked at, to occupy a surface, to pose a compositional question. The two stones can be selected from the same block for continuity, or from contrasting blocks for dialogue.

Stone & Composition

Morphe can be produced in a single stone — where the joint becomes an almost invisible precision — or in two contrasting stones, where the joint becomes a visible boundary between two characters. Both approaches are valid; both are available.
