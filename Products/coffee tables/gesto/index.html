Stone Character

The Stone
in Motion

Gesto is selected for the directional energy of its veining — lines that cross the slab like brushstrokes, giving the table an innate sense of movement even at rest. Each slab is personally reviewed at the quarry face. No two pieces share the same composition.

Design Intent

The Minimum
a Table Can Be

The design arrives from a single restraint: what is the minimum a coffee table can be? Gesto's answer is a resolved horizontal plane, its thickness calibrated to feel generous without heaviness. The result reads as effortless — which means the work was entirely in the proportions.

Yes. The standard Gesto is 130 × 65 × 36 cm, but we regularly produce pieces to client-specified dimensions. Share your requirements in your inquiry and our design team will advise on feasibility and the structural implications of the stone selected.

The Gesto is offered in polished, honed, and brushed finishes. Each finish changes the character of the stone significantly — polished intensifies the veining, honed gives a quieter matte depth, and brushed opens the grain for a more tactile, ancient quality. We can arrange sample dispatch to assist your selection.

For everyday care, wipe with a damp cloth and a pH-neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic substances such as wine, citrus, or vinegar, which can etch polished stone. We recommend sealing the surface annually with an impregnating stone sealer. Detailed care instructions are included with every piece.
