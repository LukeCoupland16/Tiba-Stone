Eclisse

organic marble coffee table

Cascata is a single, continuous marble slab that flows from horizontal seat to vertical legs in one unbroken gesture. There is no joint, no seam, no interruption — only stone in perpetual motion, frozen mid-fall.

Cascata takes its name from the Italian word for waterfall. A single Calacatta slab is precision-bent at two points, creating a continuous surface that cascades from seating plane to floor. The veining runs uninterrupted across every fold, preserving the stone's natural narrative.

The waterfall construction demands absolute mastery. The slab must be cut with invisible precision at each bend point, then mitre-joined so seamlessly that the eye perceives a single, continuous sheet of marble. The grain alignment across each corner is the signature of the maker's hand.

The slab is precision-cut at 45-degree mitres at each bend point, then bonded with colour-matched stone adhesive and reinforced internally. The result is a visually seamless fold where the veining appears to flow continuously around each corner.

Cascata is designed as a sculptural accent bench for entryways, galleries, and living spaces where brief seating is desired. For extended comfort, a bespoke cushion can be commissioned as an optional accessory.

Yes. Cascata can be made longer or wider to suit your space. Each piece is cut to order, so proportions can be adjusted during the consultation phase. Structural requirements may influence maximum span.

All Cascata benches are sealed before delivery. We recommend wiping with a damp cloth and pH-neutral stone cleaner. Resealing every 12 to 18 months preserves the surface. We provide a complete care guide with every commission.
