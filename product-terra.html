Stone as it was found. The Terra table is quarried from Travertino Romano — its warm ivory surface marked by the natural voids and fossil traces the earth left behind. Not filled, not concealed. A surface that carries geological time.

Travertino Romano

Ancient Stone,
Living Surface

Travertino Romano forms over millennia as mineral-rich spring water deposits layer upon layer of calcium carbonate. The voids and channels that run through each slab are not imperfections — they are the record of that slow, unwitnessed making.

The Architecture
of Weight

A single slab rests on two solid stone legs — no adhesive, no hidden hardware. The weight of the stone holds everything in place, as it has for centuries in the buildings of ancient Rome.

Honed produces a smooth, matte surface that diffuses light evenly. Brushed uses wire brushes to open the stone slightly, giving a more textured, antiqued feel that emphasises the fossils and mineral variation. Both are equally durable.

Travertino Romano requires minimal care. We recommend a light sealer every two to three years and wiping spills promptly with a pH-neutral cleaner. We provide a full care guide with every commission.
