Calice

marble chalice washbasin

Named for its chalice-like proportions — wider at the rim, tapering toward the base. Calice elevates the act of washing to ceremony, its flared basin catching water and light in equal measure.

Ritual Form

The Chalice,in Stone

Calice borrows from the language of ceremonial vessels — the wide-mouthed form that invites approach, the graceful taper that grounds and stabilises. In marble, this ancient proportion becomes a washbasin of quiet theatre. The flared rim provides generous space; the narrowing base occupies a surprisingly modest footprint.

Weight & Grace

Broad Above,Narrow Below

The engineering of Calice is hidden in its walls. The taper is not merely decorative — it concentrates weight at the base for stability while keeping the upper profile light and open. Water follows the natural geometry, funnelling toward the concealed drain at the lowest point.

Stone Selection

The chalice form rewards bold stone choices. Rosso Levanto transforms Calice into a jewel-toned vessel; Noir Aziza lends it a ceremonial gravitas. In lighter stones, the translucent qualities of the marble walls become visible when backlit.
