The Atelier

Stone, shaped by intention.

Tiba Stone is a bespoke marble atelier. Each piece in this catalogue is carved from a single block of stone — selected for its veining, its density, its character. We do not mass-produce. We do not stock. Every commission begins with a conversation and ends with something permanent.

This catalogue presents our current collection across ten categories. Every piece can be commissioned in your choice of marble, scaled to your specifications, and finished to your preference.

All pieces are handmade to order. Dimensions and weights are approximate. Custom sizing and stone selection available on all products.

Inquiries — contact@tibaliving.com  ·  tibastone.com
